Common Topics

Data & Privacy: Your data is stored on your own devices (and in your private iCloud if you turn on iCloud sync). We run no servers and collect no data. See our Privacy Policy for details.

iCloud Sync: Turn it on in the app under Profile > iCloud Sync, then restart the app. Make sure iCloud Drive is on for your Apple Account.

Apple Health: Manage what HybridPulse may read and write in Settings > Privacy & Security > Health > HybridPulse.

Apple Watch: Open HybridPulse on your Watch and tap Start Run. Allow location access for GPS distance and pace.

Delete your data: Profile > About HybridPulse > Delete All Data removes everything from the app (and from iCloud when sync is on).

Health disclaimer: HybridPulse is a training tool, not a medical device, and doesn't give medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting a new training program.
